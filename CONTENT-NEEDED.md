## How to update

All text and content lives in four typed files. The site has no CMS:

- `content/site.ts` — name, role, location, links (L1–L4)
- `content/projects.ts` — cards (C3/C4, L5–L8, M3 mapping)
- `content/about.ts` — biography + reveal pills (C5–C7)
- `content/experience.ts` — work history, education, and languages

Project preview generation and verification are documented in
`PREVIEWS-HANDOVER.md`. `PREVIEW-MANIFEST.md` is generated and must not be
edited by hand.
